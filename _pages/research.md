My research lies at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing** and **Deep Learning**, with a focus on making transformer-based language models work effectively for **low-resource and code-mixed languages**. I am broadly interested in **Artificial Intelligence**, **Large Language Models(LLMs)**, **Software Engineering**, and the systems and **high-performance computing** needed to train and serve the models at scale.

---

### Transformer-based Affective Computing

A central theme of my work is extracting affective and abusive signals from informal, code-mixed social media text. My undergraduate thesis proposed a **lexicon-augmented Transformer framework** that enriches pre-trained Bengali language models (e.g., BanglaBERT) with domain-specific stress lexicons mined from Bengali social media corpora.

- **Stress Identification from Bengali Social Media Texts Using Transformer-based Approaches** (Undergraduate Thesis, CUET, 2024–2025)
  - Proposed a lexicon-augmented framework that injects symbolic, domain-specific stress features into pre-trained Transformer models.
  - Showed that combining linguistic resources with neural models consistently outperforms standard fine-tuned Transformer baselines for low-resource affective computing.

### Low-Resource and Multilingual NLP

I have designed and fine-tuned BERT-based and deep learning models for several shared tasks on South Asian languages, contributing end-to-end from data collection and annotation to model development and evaluation.

- **Fake News Detection in Malayalam** — designed and fine-tuned BERT-based models for detecting fake news in Malayalam social media texts. Published at *DravidianLangTech @ NAACL 2025*.
- **Hate Speech Detection in Devanagari-script Languages** — built deep learning pipelines for script-language identification and hate speech detection, achieving over 85% classification accuracy across multiple language families. Published at *CHiPSAL @ COLING 2025*.
- **Multimodal Misogyny Meme Detection** — developed multimodal fusion architectures combining visual and textual features for misogyny meme classification. Published at *DravidianLangTech @ NAACL 2025*.
